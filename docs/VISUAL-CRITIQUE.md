## Fourteen passes

1. **Idea:** the timber volume is immediate; the physical console is the principal interaction.
2. **Hierarchy:** one two-line name, named chapters, concise room facts, a large equipment feature and practical inquiry.
3. **Specificity:** real studio images, the photographed custom console, actual staff and the official studio Instagram account.
4. **Type:** self-hosted Caslon/Chivo; uppercase is the requested identity. Body/notes use readable roles.
5. **Color:** timber, neutral stone, bone and brass; the native Instagram surface keeps its own design.
6. **Composition:** vertical room index, broad console, asymmetrical instrument/portrait/gallery placement, aligned contact and common utility shell.
7. **Rhythm:** chapters vary composition while sharing gutters and spacing; no repeated feature-card row.
8. **Imagery:** real photographs; portrait focal points preserve faces. No generated or stock studio image.
9. **Motion:** bounded progress opening, native-scroll image travel, brief entrances and input-driven 3D; idle reading motion yields to interaction.
10. **Controls:** open text actions, a coherent stroked SVG family, native fields/disclosures, clear focus and real fallbacks.
11. **Fullness:** images, equipment and useful text close the frame; the legal page is a deliberately readable document.
12. **Substance:** room dimensions, API model, inventory, credited session, people, live profile and contact are present.
13. **Collisions:** final standard and narrow renders show no clipped headline or face. Narrow gallery actions wrap; the compact contact email avoids a terminal-letter orphan.
14. **Competitor/live thing:** the console and building evidence are specific, though large photographic type is an established editorial approach rather than an unprecedented invention.
