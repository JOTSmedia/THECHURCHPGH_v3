Privacy.

Inquiries, preferences & embedded media.

Project inquiries

The inquiry builder prepares a message in your browser. Your entries are not submitted to a website server, stored in browser storage, or sent automatically. Choosing “Open email app” passes the message to your email application. Your email provider handles delivery when you choose to send.

Copying or downloading a message happens on your device. Contact the studio at info@thechurchrecordingstudio.com for questions about correspondence sent to the studio.

Local preferences

If you pause animation, this website remembers that preference in local storage on your device. Your browser’s reduced-motion setting is also respected. No inquiry data is stored by the site.

External websites

The Instagram profile widget connects to Instagram as it nears the viewport. The performance video connects to YouTube’s privacy-enhanced player only when you choose “Play session.” These services receive request information and may apply their own cookies and privacy policies. External profile, reference and Google Maps pages open only when you follow their links. Photos, fonts and the 3D library are hosted with this website, rather than loaded from third-party CDNs.

Website hosting

The hosting provider may process technical request information, such as IP addresses and browser details, to deliver the website. When deployed on GitHub Pages, GitHub’s privacy statement applies to that hosting.
